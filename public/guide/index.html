Students

You use the class library on the shared tablet. It takes about ten seconds. You never need a password.

Borrow a book

  1. Hold the book’s back-cover barcode in the box on the screen. It beeps and the cover appears.
  2. Tap your name. If the class is large, tap your first letter in the A–Z bar first.
  3. Tap your emoji.
  4. Tap Borrow. Write down nothing; the due date shows on screen.

Return a book

Scan the book, tap your name and emoji, then tap Return. Then you can say whether you finished it, give 1–5 stars, pick an emoji and say if you recommend it.

Badges and streaks

A book counts toward badges only if you returned it, marked finished, and kept it at least 24 hours. The streak counts weeks (Monday start) in which you finished a book. The screen also suggests other books in this class library you may like.

My books

Tap My books, then your name and emoji, to see what you have, due dates, your badges and streak.

If something is blocked

You will see “Please see your teacher” if you have 3 books out or one is overdue. Return the overdue book, or ask your teacher.

No barcode, or it won’t scan

Use Search by cover or Type the title.

Class librarian

The class librarian is a pupil helper. You can see problems, sync and undo a mistake. You cannot delete, export or change rules.

Open the librarian screen

  1. Tap the 🔒 at the top right.
  2. Enter the librarian PIN.

Teacher PIN

If a blocked pupil must borrow anyway, only the teacher enters the teacher PIN. The override is recorded.

No Wi-Fi

Everything still works. Actions are saved on the tablet and sync automatically when the tablet is online. “Waiting to sync” should reach 0.

If the same problem keeps appearing, tell the teacher.

Teacher (admin)

Teachers manage the classes they are assigned. Sign in at /admin (Cloudflare Access email code, or the admin key).

First-time setup checklist

  1. + New class.
  2. Kiosk & PINs: set librarian and teacher PINs (6+ digits).
  3. Pupils: paste names, one per line.
  4. Books: scan, type ISBNs or import Excel.
  5. Open the kiosk link once on the tablet; Add to Home Screen; turn on Guided Access (iPad) or screen pinning (Android).

Needs attention (home)

Lists what to do: overdue books, lost books, titles or covers missing, returns that matched no loan, a tablet not seen or unsynced for 3+ days.

Dashboard

Books

Add by scanning or typing an ISBN, or import an Excel sheet (ISBN column as text). Edit title, author, copies and withdrawn status inline.

Pupils

Reports

Overdue, on loan, lost, popular, never borrowed, per pupil, history. Export CSV.

Lost and found

Mark a loan lost (the borrower is kept, and it stops counting toward their limit). Mark found to restore it.

Year end

Archive class permanently deletes pupil names, loans and responses for that class.

Supervisor

A supervisor oversees all classes: creates classes, adds teachers and assigns them, and sees activity everywhere.

Teachers

Add a teacher’s email and tick their classes. They sign in with Cloudflare Access and see only those classes. Remove them here too.

Activity

An audit log of overrides, lost/found, PIN and token changes.

The ADMIN_KEY secret is a break-glass supervisor login. Keep it private.

Help and privacy

Barcode won’t scan
Use good light, hold 15–25 cm away, steady for a second. Only the publisher’s ISBN barcode is read. Otherwise search by cover.
“Waiting to sync” won’t clear
Check Wi-Fi, tap Sync now. If a kiosk link was revoked, reopen the new link.
No title found for an ISBN
Type it in from the Books tab.
Lost tablet or leaked link
Kiosk & PINs → rotate token.

Privacy: only first name and last initial are shown. No photos or free text from pupils are stored.
